## Voice preservation and approximation

All seven audition MP3s passed complete decoding before being shared, and the owner
approved each. The final physical body, voice, pitch, phone, melody and articulation plans
match the prepared scores; only common mix headroom trims differ. Spin's second opening
entry was corrected against the source outside the approved excerpts; the instrumental
rest has a regression check and the approved excerpt plans stay unchanged.

The original singer is not replayed in any generated voice channel. The physical airway
renders the words from source-aligned dictionary phones, complete consonants, connected
vowels, late diphthong glides and measured melody. Remember's two densely layered closing
call passages use estimated source phonetics, heard and approved in its audition.
Pronunciation, pitch recovery, timing, harmony layers, scenery and likenesses are approximate.
Faint separation traces may remain in the original instrumental/background channel.
