# KATSEYE — Gnarly

The complete 2:22 music-video edit plays at `?scene=gnarly`. Three CHIMERA airways reproduce the spoken opener, rhythmic verse and group chants over the original separated backing and retained effects. The six stylized performers share these physical voices. Faint vocal traces can remain after separation.

The 119-cut sequence follows the pink grocery tray, sandwich assembly, red fridge corridor, green club and crowd surf, blue limousine, mansion crash, and red-carpet finale. The costumes and lighting change with each set. Cody Critcheloe directed the original video. [Official music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R2-yomhYAj4).

Run `bun tools/render-gnarly-scene.ts` with FFmpeg to rebuild the three physical voices, mix and 20 Hz mouth movement from the prepared backing and score. After listening, run `bun run content:manifest`. Raw source media and analysis files stay outside the project.
